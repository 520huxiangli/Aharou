# Home-screen Widget

Put Aharou's quick entries and recent sessions on your home screen, without opening the app first.

## Add & Remove

Long-press an empty spot on the home screen → open "Widgets" → find Aharou → drag "**Quick entries & recent sessions**" onto the screen.

- A 4×2 cell size works best; you can resize it horizontally and vertically, but too small a cell will clip the content.
- To remove it, drag it to "Remove" like any ordinary icon.

The name of this entry differs slightly between launchers — usually under "Widgets", or the "Add to home screen" menu after a long press.

## What It Shows

Three quick buttons along the top:

- **New chat**: start a new session and open the chat page.
- **Open chat**: open the app and return to the chat page.
- **Voice call**: open the app and start a voice call right away.

Below them, up to five **most recently updated sessions** (across workspaces, excluding subagent sessions), each showing the session title and a relative time (e.g. "5 minutes ago"). Tap one to jump into that session. When there are none, "No recent sessions" is shown.

## Refresh & Theme

- The widget refreshes automatically when it is **added to the home screen, after a device reboot, and after an app update**, and also **every 30 minutes** (the shortest interval the system allows — the app keeps no background service for this).
- Its colors follow the app's current look (preset color scheme / Monet color extraction / light and dark mode).
- You can place several widget instances on one home screen; they are independent, and opening a session from one does not affect another.

## Missing Content or Slow to Update

- The sessions in the widget come from the chat history stored on the phone; if you have not chatted yet it is naturally empty, and sessions appear at the next refresh once you have.
- To see the newest sessions immediately, remove the widget and add it again — that refreshes it right away.
- Only the few **most recently active** sessions are listed; older ones are not, so open the app and use the sidebar session list to find them.
